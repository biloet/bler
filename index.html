FAQ

Got questions?

What is BLER?

BLER turns your X or fomo profile into a live market on Robinhood Chain. Your fans can buy and sell your token anytime, and you earn from every trade.

What is Pons V2?

Pons V2 is the token launchpad protocol on Robinhood Chain that BLER is built on. It runs the ETH bonding curve, the anti-bot launch protection, and graduation to Uniswap V4.

Is it non-custodial?

Yes. You trade directly from your own wallet. BLER never takes custody of your assets.

What is a bonding curve?

A pricing formula where the token price rises as supply is bought and falls as it is sold. On BLER the curve is priced in ETH, and when it sells out the token moves to a Uniswap V4 pool.

How do I earn as a creator?

Every trade on your token pays a creator fee in ETH straight to your wallet.
