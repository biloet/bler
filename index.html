FAQ

Questions?

What is BLER?

BLER turns your social profile into a tradable market. Anyone can buy and sell your profile token on a bonding curve.

Is it non-custodial?

Yes. You trade directly from your own wallet. BLER never takes custody of your assets.

What is a bonding curve?

A pricing formula where the token price increases as supply is bought, and decreases as it is sold — always liquid, always transparent.

How do I earn as a creator?

Every trade on your profile pays a small creator fee straight to your wallet.
